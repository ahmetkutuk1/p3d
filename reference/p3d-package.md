# p3d: Interactive 3D Visualization of p-Value Surfaces

Provides interactive three-dimensional visualization of p-value surfaces
for single-sample t-tests. Enables simultaneous exploration of effect
size, sample size, and significance level, making the continuous nature
of p-values and the moderating effect of sample size visually apparent.
Includes both interactive (rgl, plotly) and static (persp) rendering
options.
